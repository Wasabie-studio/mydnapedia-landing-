Our flagship MDP 360° report now covers 100+ health traits across disease risk, nutrition, fitness, skin and hair, mental wellbeing and more.

Our flagship MDP 360° report now covers more than 100 health traits, spanning disease risk, nutrition, fitness, skin and hair, mental wellbeing, and pharmacogenomics — how your body responds to the medicines your doctor prescribes.

The expansion means a single cheek swab now gives a fuller picture of your biology, and because your DNA doesn't change, that one report keeps paying off for years.

As the science advances, we'll continue adding new insights to existing reports at no extra cost — so the value of your test grows over time.
