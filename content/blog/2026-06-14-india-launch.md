We officially opened bookings across India. Every plan begins with the same simple cheek swab you do at home, followed by a detailed report and a one-on-one session with a qualified genetic counsellor.

For years, the kind of deep genetic insight we offer was reserved for elite athletes and high-level medical research. Bringing it to everyday families across India means someone in Delhi worried about diabetes, or a runner in Mumbai trying to understand their training, can finally get answers they didn't know they were missing.

Every report is written to be understood, not decoded. Before you ever see a single number, a qualified genetic counsellor walks you through what your results mean and, just as importantly, what you can actually do about them.

This is only the beginning. Over the coming months we'll be expanding into more cities, running community counselling camps, and adding new insights to our reports as the science advances.
