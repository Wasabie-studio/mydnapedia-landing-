Over one weekend, our counsellors met more than 200 families to explain how genetic wellness reports work and answer questions one-on-one.

Across two days, our counsellors sat down with more than two hundred families to talk through how genetic wellness testing works — what a report can reveal, what it can't, and why a one-on-one session matters as much as the test itself.

The most common question we heard was a simple one: "What do I actually do with this?" That's exactly the gap our counselling is built to close, translating raw genetic data into clear, practical steps around nutrition, fitness and long-term disease risk.

Thank you to everyone who came and made the weekend what it was. We'll be announcing the next round of camps soon — follow along here for dates and cities.
