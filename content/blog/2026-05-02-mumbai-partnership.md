A new partnership with a Mumbai wellness network brings genetic wellness testing and counselling closer to more people.

The new partnership brings MyDNAPedia's testing and counselling into an established Mumbai wellness network, so more people can book a test and meet a genetic counsellor closer to home.

For us, partnerships like this are the fastest way to make preventive, personalised health the norm rather than the exception, reaching communities through trust and familiar local relationships instead of advertising.

We're actively speaking with partners in other cities. If you'd like to bring MyDNAPedia to your community, we'd love to hear from you.
