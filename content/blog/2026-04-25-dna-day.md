To mark World DNA Day, we opened our lab and walked visitors through the full journey of a sample, from swab to finished report.

To mark World DNA Day, we opened our lab and walked visitors through the full journey of a sample, from the moment a cheek swab arrives to the finished, counsellor-reviewed report.

Seeing the process first-hand tends to answer the questions people didn't know they had: how their privacy is protected, how results are validated, and why a single test stays relevant for the rest of their life.

Thanks to everyone who visited. We plan to make the open house a regular event. Details for the next one will be posted here.
