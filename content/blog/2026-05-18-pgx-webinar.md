Our scientific team walked attendees through pharmacogenomics — how your genes influence the way your body responds to the medicines your doctor prescribes.

Pharmacogenomics looks at how your genes influence the way your body responds to medicines — why a standard dose works well for one person and poorly for another. It's one of the most practical, and most overlooked, parts of a genetic wellness report.

In the session, our scientific team walked through real examples of how this information can help you and your doctor make better-informed choices, followed by a live Q&A with attendees.
